## Four review perspectives

**Parsing:** right-aligned dates originally extracted after surrounding paragraphs. Dates now sit beside titles in the reading flow. Automatic word hyphenation is disabled. Three text extraction modes must find every intended segment in order. This is local PDF QA, not commercial ATS certification.

**Recruiter:** undergraduate degree, graduation and grade are at the top. SWE exposes the Build Sprint win and programming contribution early. Research versions lead with relevant investigations and label the university project. Removed general self-praise and long unrelated experience blocks. No photo or proficiency bars in PDFs.

**Technical specialist:** distinguish using Hugging Face components from inventing an architecture; integrating MediaPipe from developing or validating a pose-estimation model; tutorial ray tracing from original graphics research. Classifier preprocessing occurs before splits, so no headline accuracy is used. Current Battle Tower code is not asserted to be the exact winning submission. No claim of verified deployed cloud behavior or medically validated exercise recognition.

**Skeptical editor:** retained only concrete actions and methods. The first-place award is supported by the supplied organizer announcement; team size and programmer contribution by the owner. Historical 475-test count, 97% accuracy, cohort rank, GPA conversion, unsupported volunteering scope and generic “deep understanding” language were removed or narrowed. Model diagnostics are described without claiming benchmark improvements.

The documents were reviewed again after layout changes. Research competitiveness remains constrained by actual experience: no confirmed publication, lab placement, or controlled generative-model benchmark. Clear writing exposes the available evidence; it cannot replace missing research accomplishments.
