# Recruiting evidence and editorial decisions

Reviewed 17 November 2026. This report separates documented product behavior, advice, and implementation choices. Postings can close without notice; these links are research examples, not a guarantee of current availability or personal eligibility.

## ATS findings

- **Documented parsing:** [Greenhouse unsuccessful parsing](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/200989175-Unsuccessful-resume-parse) describes candidate-field autofill, a 2.5 MB parser limit, and risks from image-only files, columns, tables, headers/footers and unclear sections. Failure can leave the file attached for manual processing. It does not establish automatic rejection for all failed parses.
- [Lever resume parsing](https://help.lever.co/s/article/Understanding-Resume-Parsing) describes extracting information such as identity and contact details into a profile. Its indexed official text was accessible, but the page itself returned a loading/CSS error. No additional undocumented Lever behavior is assumed.
- **Documented searches:** [Greenhouse keyword search](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/115004600186-Search-resumes-for-keywords) lets recruiters search candidate text. Clear contextual terminology helps discoverability; repeating words is not evidence of skill.
- **Documented application rules:** [Greenhouse auto-reject](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/360000653472-Auto-reject) uses configured application-question responses. This differs from PDF parsing or resume keyword search.
- **Documented AI review:** [Ashby AI-assisted application review](https://docs.ashbyhq.com/ai-assisted-application-review) evaluates employer-defined criteria, reports meets/does-not-meet/undecided, and supports sorting by the proportion of criteria met. It is configured by employers. This is not a universal ATS score, and the documentation does not establish that every employer enables it or automatically rejects a particular percentage.
- **Engineering inference:** selectable text, Unicode mappings, ordinary headings and simple reading order reduce avoidable extraction ambiguity. Local extraction does not replicate commercial field extraction, recruiter judgment, or employer configuration. No ATS compatibility percentage is reported.

## Advice and conflicting recommendations

[MIT resumes](https://capd.mit.edu/resources/resumes/) supports concise, position-specific undergraduate resumes, contextual technical skills and class/personal projects. [MIT's checklist](https://capd.mit.edu/resources/resume-checklist/) also recommends Word and avoiding templates. That broad prescription is career advice, not a controlled test proving that all templates or LaTeX fail. Here the actual PDF output is tested instead.

[Harvard's strong-resume guide](https://careerservices.fas.harvard.edu/resources/create-a-strong-resume/) emphasizes relevant evidence and checking PDF conversion. Its full page was intermittently rate-limited; indexed official guidance was used conservatively. [Penn's STEM CV guidance](https://careerservices.upenn.edu/stemcv/) distinguishes a concise undergraduate resume from a fuller academic record, with sections determined by real experience. It notes that undergraduate academic CVs can be two pages; that is not a reason to pad this profile.

Defaults here: one page, no photograph or personal demographics, readable type, A4 for the canonical European-facing documents and optional Letter exports. These are design choices, not universal European/North American rules. The posting's instructions take priority. [EPFL](https://summer.epfl.ch/apply.html) asks applicants to retain their institutional grading scale. [ETH SSRF](https://inf.ethz.ch/studies/summer-research-fellowship/how-to-apply.html) specifically requests an English Europass CV and official transcripts; the generic research CV is not automatically compliant with that format. No work authorization is inferred from residence, nationality, or degree location.

The four versions therefore change evidence selection, not the underlying facts.

## Changes and limits

Replaced seven lightly differentiated historical variants with four focused canonical versions. Added the user-confirmed Build Sprint win and programmer contribution. Removed Learning Website from CVs and reduced it to a minor portfolio link. Corrected classifier provenance and unsupported accuracy, clarified course/tutorial origins, and removed unverifiable current test counts. Fixed actual date-reading-order and mobile overflow defects found during validation.

A grade and expected graduation were confirmed by the owner; no transcript was audited. Employment/volunteering and language proficiency remain self-reported historical material, not externally certified. Existing experience stays on the portfolio but is omitted from the short CVs to prioritize stronger technical evidence. No publications, academic rank, professional research role, immigration status, measured production scale or new model benchmarks were established. No employer was contacted and no application was submitted.
